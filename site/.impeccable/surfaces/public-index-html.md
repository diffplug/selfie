## Direction contract

THESIS: The visitor stands where the robot stands, holding up the phone, and a coding agent checks its own work by taking a selfie. Refuses the framed app screenshot beside a headline.

OWN-WORLD: One flat sky blue (#bfd9f0), ink outlines, yellow button, handwritten pink note. A photoreal matte-white ceramic and dark-metal robot, with a phone camera interface on its screen.

STORY: The visitor sees yesterday's and today's selfies differ by one recolored plate, understands that this is what Selfie catches, and either starts from GitHub or joins the list through the shutter.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Copy in the left column. Portrait phone right of center, held by a robot arm entering from the lower right corner, thumb at the shutter. "Yesterday" print fanned at the phone's upper right with a pink note. Primary actions: the yellow GitHub button and the shutter.

FORM: Phone front camera, seventh on the ordered list, seed:a81eb6e7e3cedd89.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Decided with the owner

- Portrait selfie only, with the real arm coming from the viewer's side and the phone screen as a mirror.
- Hand pose follows the owner's photos.
- The change is a color change on one head plate, red yesterday and blue today, and nothing else differs.
- Style B was chosen because it fits next to the GitHub Primer app.
